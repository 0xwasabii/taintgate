    Decision log

    Policy

    First matching rule wins. Tools that match nothing are treated as harmless and allowed (and logged).

    • source: its results are untrusted. Reading one taints the session and records the emails, URLs, hosts and paths it contained.
    • sink, strict: gated whenever the session is tainted.
    • sink, flow: gated only if the call's arguments contain something that came from untrusted content and was not in your own prompt.
    • ask / deny: what happens on a hit. Codex only supports deny, so ask becomes deny there.
